Native CoreAudio HAL : Zero Drivers

Play audio through two outputs at the same time.

A native macOS menu bar app that lets you stream audio across two or more output devices simultaneously with one click. Share a movie with two pairs of AirPods, play through speakers and headphones in sync, or stream without third-party audio drivers.

Engineering Architecture

Zero Audio Drivers
Built entirely on Apple CoreAudio HAL APIs (AudioHardwareCreateAggregateDevice with kAudioAggregateDeviceIsStackedKey = 1). No kernel extensions, no virtual soundcards, and zero third-party kext security prompts.
Hardware Drift Compensation
Automatically enables CoreAudio hardware drift correction when pairing Bluetooth headsets with wired or built-in speakers, preventing audio desynchronization over long sessions.
Independent Volume Sliders
Controls the gain of each audio device in the multi-output mix independently, letting you balance headphones against loud room speakers seamlessly.
Global Hotkey Toggle
Press ⌥⌘D anywhere in macOS to toggle multi-output mode without opening the menu bar extra.
Instant Disconnect Recovery
If AirPods go into the case or a monitor disconnects, DuoSound instantly restores your previous default audio output and destroys the aggregate device without dropped samples.
